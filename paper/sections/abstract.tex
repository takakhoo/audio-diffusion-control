\begin{abstract}
A slider is a low-rank update to a frozen generative model whose strength is set at inference time, so one prompt and seed can be moved along a single axis. Sliders for music are scored by text--audio embedding similarity, on axes someone chose by writing a prompt pair. We measure what that leaves out, on Stable Audio Open and ACE-Step: whether a waveform property follows the slider, whether two unrelated quality predictors still rate the result as music, how much of the piece survives, and what else moves. A trained slider matches prompt-pair guidance with a third of the forward passes, and prompt interpolation fails. Two sliders pass the embedding test and fail the waveform test. We then take axes from real music: independent components of embeddings of \covReal{} recordings include arousal and valence, and the model's output spans \covLo{}--\covHi{}\% of the real spread along them. Sliders along these axes, trained from the axis's own tags or with no text from the model's clips, follow them on held-out prompts (rank correlation up to 0.91) and carry a clip across the real-music mean, 2.4 real-music standard deviations inside the usable span. Principal axes of the generator's own activations describe production instead.
\end{abstract}
