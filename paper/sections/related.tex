\section{Related work}

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Sliders.} Concept Sliders \citep{gandikota2024sliders} train a LoRA \citep{hu2022lora} whose output matches the frozen model's prediction shifted along the difference between a positive-prompt and a negative-prompt prediction. SliderSpace \citep{gandikota2025sliderspace} removes the prompt pair: it takes principal components of CLIP embeddings of many images of one concept and trains one adapter per component. Measured Sliders \citep{chen2026measured} define image sliders by differentiable measurements and hold other measurements still; we adopt their selectivity score.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Steering audio models.} FreeSliders \citep{ezra2025freesliders} apply the Concept Sliders target at inference time without training, on image, video, and audio models; most of their audio concepts are sound effects. TADA \citep{staniszewski2026tada} steers cross-attention activations of audio diffusion transformers, with steering vectors from prompt pairs or from sparse-autoencoder features selected with prompt pairs. Facchiano et al.~\citep{facchiano2025patching} compute difference-in-means vectors for tempo and timbre, AnchorSteer \citep{chang2026anchorsteer} trains text-free concept modules on self-generated clips, and Singh et al.~\citep{singh2025discovering} find sparse features in autoregressive music models. These score steering by text--audio alignment and perceptual distance to the unsteered output. TADA checks two concepts against a descriptor, and Wang \citep{wang2026counterfactual} shows with a key estimator and a beat tracker that apparent agreement with an instruction can be the model's prior. None scores every control against a waveform property fixed in advance, compares against signal processing, or reports what else moves.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Where axes come from.} In image diffusion, principal components of a U-Net's bottleneck activations are semantic \citep{kwon2023hspace,haas2023directions}. Guinot et al.~\citep{guinot2026steering} learn sparse features of music embeddings to steer retrieval, and independent components of text embeddings are known to be more interpretable than principal ones \citep{yamagiwa2023ica}. We found no work that takes axes from embeddings of real music and trains generator controls along them.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Diversity against real music.} Recall and coverage in an embedding space give one number per model \citep{novack2024presto}. Slendebroek and Metaxa \citep{slendebroek2026flattening} compare two commercial systems with human recordings on hand-built signal features and report homogenisation. Our comparison is per axis, on axes learned from real recordings, for open models.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Effects with known parameters.} RIME \citep{schaffer2026rime} builds paired edit data by applying effects with known parameters. We use such effects as a baseline that a learned control has to justify itself against.
