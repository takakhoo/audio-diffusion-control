\section{Related work}

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Sliders for image diffusion.} Concept Sliders \citep{gandikota2024sliders} train a LoRA \citep{hu2022lora} whose output matches the frozen model's prediction shifted along the difference between a positive-prompt and a negative-prompt prediction. SliderSpace \citep{gandikota2025sliderspace} removes the prompt pair: it takes principal components of CLIP embeddings of many images generated for one concept and trains one rank-one LoRA per component, using a few-step distilled model so the embedding loss can see a clean image after a single denoising step.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Sliders and steering for audio.} FreeSliders \citep{ezra2025freesliders} apply the Concept Sliders target at inference time without training, on image, video, and audio models including Stable Audio Open; most of their audio concepts are sound effects. TADA \citep{staniszewski2026tada} steers activations in audio diffusion transformers and reports that concepts concentrate in a few attention layers; it includes Concept Sliders as a baseline. Facchiano et al.~\citep{facchiano2025patching} compute difference-in-means steering vectors for tempo and timbre in a music model. All three score steering by text--audio alignment in a learned embedding and by perceptual distance to the unsteered output. None reports whether a measured property of the waveform follows the control, none compares against plain signal processing, and none reports what else moves when a control moves.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Effects with known parameters.} RIME \citep{schaffer2026rime} builds paired edit data for music post-production by applying effects with known parameters. We borrow the idea in a different role: effects with known parameters are a baseline that a learned control has to justify itself against.
