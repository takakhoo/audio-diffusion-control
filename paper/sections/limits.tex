\section{Limits}

No listening test was run; every claim about quality rests on a learned predictor and every claim about meaning on CLAP tags and signal descriptors. Descriptors are proxies: energy decay time stands in for reverberation and is confounded by sustained instruments, and estimated tempo suffers octave errors. Clips are ten seconds long. The usable-span tolerance of 0.5 CE is a choice, and a different tolerance moves the reported spans. The set-contrast trainer's cancellation argument is first-order; correlated properties of the two sets are learned together, which the leakage matrix shows. Discovered axes are labelled after the fact by the tags they move, and a different tag vocabulary would produce different labels for the same direction.
