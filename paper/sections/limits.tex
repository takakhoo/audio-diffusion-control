\section{Limits}

No listening test was run. Quality rests on two learned predictors and meaning on tags and signal descriptors, which are proxies: energy decay time is confounded by sustained instruments, and SongEval was trained on songs with vocals. Most clips are ten seconds long. The usable-span tolerance of 0.5 CE is a choice. Coverage depends on our prompt list and on FMA as the sample of real music, and both embedding models were trained on real recordings. Axis labels come from an 80-word vocabulary and a different vocabulary would name the same direction differently. The internal-axis result is for token-averaged cross-attention outputs of an eight-step distilled model.
