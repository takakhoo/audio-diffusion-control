\section{Measurement}

For each prompt and seed the same initial noise is rendered at every slider position; we call this a trajectory. All numbers use 24 prompts held out from training, three seeds, and ten-second clips unless stated.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Does a measured property follow the slider?} Each slider is paired in advance with a descriptor computed from the waveform: spectral centroid, onset rate, beat-tracked tempo \citep{foscarin2024beatthis}, energy decay time, percussive energy share, low-band and stereo-side energy ratios, key clarity, major-minus-minor key fit, harmonic change rate. We report the mean Spearman correlation between position and descriptor over trajectories ($\rho$) and the change in units of the descriptor's standard deviation across unsteered clips.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Does a second model agree?} The projection of each clip on the slider's text direction is computed in CLAP and again in MuQ-MuLan, which shares no weights or training data with it.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Does it still sound like music?} Every clip is scored by Audiobox Aesthetics \citep{tjandra2025aesthetics} (content enjoyment, CE, 1--10) and by SongEval \citep{yao2025songeval} (musicality, 1--5), a head on MuQ features trained on human ratings of generated songs. Both are anchored by 2{,}000 excerpts of real recordings. The \emph{usable span} is the set of positions, walking outward from zero, over which mean CE stays within 0.5 of the unsteered clips. Effect sizes are reported inside it.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{How much of the piece survives?} CLAP similarity between each clip and the same trajectory at position zero.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{What else moves?} The slope of every descriptor against position, in standard deviations per unit: a leakage matrix. A tag panel of 80 instrument, genre, mood, and character words, scored in CLAP, puts the change in words.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Coverage.} For a direction $d$ found in real recordings, the coverage of a model is the standard deviation of its clips' projections on $d$ divided by that of the recordings. We report it over all prompts, and after removing each prompt's mean from the generated clips and each genre's mean from the recordings. A slider's reach along $d$ is reported in the same unit.
