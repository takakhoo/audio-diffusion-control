\section{Measurement}

For each prompt and seed we render the same initial noise at every slider position and call the result a trajectory. All reported numbers use 24 evaluation prompts held out from training.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Does a measured property follow the slider?} Each slider is paired in advance with a descriptor computed from the waveform with no learned model: spectral centroid, onset rate, pulse clarity, energy decay time, percussive energy share, low-band and stereo-side energy ratios, key clarity, major-minus-minor key fit, harmonic change rate, and level dynamics. We report the mean Spearman correlation between position and descriptor over trajectories ($\rho$), the share of trajectories whose two ends are ordered as intended, and the change in the descriptor in units of its standard deviation across unsteered clips.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Does it still sound like music?} Every clip is scored by the Audiobox Aesthetics predictor \citep{tjandra2025aesthetics}. We use content enjoyment (CE) on its 1--10 scale and anchor it with 2{,}000 ten-second excerpts of real recordings from FMA \citep{defferrard2017fma}. The \emph{usable span} of a slider is the set of positions, walking outward from zero, over which mean CE stays within 0.5 of the unsteered clips. Effect sizes are reported inside the usable span, which stops a slider from being credited for change it only achieves by destroying the clip.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{What does it change, in words?} We embed every clip with CLAP \citep{wu2023clap} and score it against 80 tags for instruments, genres, moods, and playing characteristics. For a slider we report the tags whose similarity rises and falls most between its two ends, and the rank correlation between position and the projection on the slider's text direction. The same correlation is computed with MuQ-MuLan as a check that does not depend on CLAP.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{How much of the piece survives?} CLAP similarity, frame-wise chroma similarity, and onset-envelope correlation between each clip and the same trajectory at position zero.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{What else moves?} The slope of every descriptor against position, in standard deviations per unit, for every slider: a leakage matrix.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Baselines.} Under identical prompts, seeds, and sampler: the training target applied directly at every step (the training-free approach of FreeSliders, at three times the compute), interpolation of the text conditioning toward $c^+$ or $c^-$, and, where one exists, a conventional effect applied to the unsteered clip (spectral tilt, low shelf, mid/side gain, convolution reverb, soft clipping, band-limiting).
