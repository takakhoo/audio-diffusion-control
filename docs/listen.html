Listening test

About ten minutes, with headphones if you have them. You hear short clips of generated music and answer one question about each pair. There are no right answers to study for: the point is to find out whether people hear what the measurements say.

Before you start

Each clip is ten seconds long. Listen to both clips in a pair as often as you like, then answer.

Nothing is uploaded. Your answers stay in this browser tab until you download them at the end, and it is up to you whether to send the file to anyone. No name or contact detail is asked for.
